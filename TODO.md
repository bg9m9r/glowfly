# Glowfly backlog

The backlog has moved to GitHub:

- **Planned work** is tracked as [issues](https://github.com/bg9m9r/glowfly/issues) on the
  [Glowfly Project](https://github.com/users/bg9m9r/projects/2) (columns Inbox, Ready, In
  progress, In review, Done), the source of truth for priorities and status. Milestones and
  their exit criteria are [GitHub milestones](https://github.com/bg9m9r/glowfly/milestones);
  [ROADMAP.md](ROADMAP.md) holds only the goal and scope.
- **Ideas** (untriaged polish, edge cases and follow-ups) live as [`idea`-labelled issues](https://github.com/bg9m9r/glowfly/issues?q=label%3Aidea)
  in the Project's Backlog; [docs/ideas](docs/ideas/README.md) says how to add, promote or drop one.
- **History**: `git log` and the closed issues.
